## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, authoritative, and calm aesthetic tailored for enterprise-grade AI and intelligent systems engineering. It balances bleeding-edge deep tech capability with the sober, dependable architecture required by SMB and B2B leaders. The visual signature merges high-contrast minimalism with subtle luminescent elements—reminiscent of optical computing, acoustic resonance, and cleanroom precision.

### Design Movements & Visual Language
- **Minimalist Precision:** Generous negative space, restrained structural lines, and deliberate information pacing to communicate clarity over hype.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Restrained cyan-to-ultramarine gradients and ambient glow effects mimic coherent light signals across deep void backdrops.
- **Architectonic Structure:** Crisp, low-contrast hairline dividers paired with a subtle, repeating hexagonal nodal lattice evoke structured neural pipelines and engineered physical systems without visual noise.

## Colors

The palette leverages a dual-substrate model: a commanding deep navy void for focal hero areas, command dashboards, and grounding footers, transitioning seamlessly into crisp, warm-stone light fields for text-heavy content, case studies, and analytical documentation.

### Substrates & Backgrounds
- **Primary Void (Hero, Canvas, Dark Surfaces):** `#001029` (Deep Navy)
- **Deep Void Shading:** `#000B1C`
- **Surface Elevation Dark:** `#00183A`
- **Light Field (Content Sections, Documentation):** `#FAFAF9` (Warm Clean Stone)
- **Light Field Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF`

### Core Accents & Interactive States
- **Primary Tech Accent:** `#0A6CFF` (Precision Royal Cobalt)
- **Secondary Luminescent Accent:** `#00E5FF` (Electric Cyan)
- **Highlight Blue:** `#0AA5FF` (Mid-tone Spectral Blue)
- **Interactive Hover State:** `#0047C7` (Concentrated Deep Blue)
- **Primary Kinetic Gradient:** Linear sweep from `#00E5FF` (0%) to `#0A6CFF` (100%) at an angle of 135deg.

### Typography & Neutrals
- **Ink Primary (Light Surfaces):** `#0B0F19`
- **Ink Secondary (Light Surfaces):** `#6B7280`
- **Ink Muted / Borders (Light Surfaces):** `#E5E7EB`
- **Ink Primary (Dark Surfaces):** `#F9FAFB`
- **Ink Secondary (Dark Surfaces):** `#94A3B8`
- **Hairline Grid Border (Dark Surfaces):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

## Typography

Typography pairs technical geometric display forms with an obsessively balanced, human-readable body engine. 

- **Display & Headings:** `Space Grotesk` is selected to provide structural, technological punch with geometric precision. Subheadings and eyebrow labels take wide letter-spacing (`0.15em`) in full uppercase, reinforcing scientific rigor.
- **Reading & Interactivity:** `Inter` handles operational flow, structured specs, and continuous reading, calibrated strictly at a 1.6 line-height multiplier (`26px` on `16px` font) to guarantee calm cognitive scanning during data-heavy reviews.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a fluid 12-column grid constrained within a maximum width of `1280px` for structured readability.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `4rem` (`64px`) page margin. Content modules span symmetrical blocks (4, 6, 8, 12).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, `2rem` (`32px`) page margin. Multi-column cards collapse to 2-up configurations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1.25rem` (`20px`) canvas margins. Side-by-side elements collapse cleanly into single-column vertical stacks.

### Rhythm & Pacing
Vertical spacing maintains a strict 8px harmonic factor. Section separators require ample rhythm (`5rem` to `7rem` distance) to enforce an uncluttered, premium corporate poise.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established not through heavy skeuomorphism, but through translucent stacking, controlled specular reflections, and ambient photonic diffusion.

### Dark Surface Depths
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#001029`.
- **Level 1 (Structural Card):** `#00183A` backed by an ultra-thin 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).
- **Level 2 (Hover & Modal Tiers):** `#00204D` with an ambient glow: `0px 12px 36px -8px rgba(10, 108, 255, 0.22), 0px 0px 1px 1px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.3)`.

### Light Surface Depths
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#FAFAF9`.
- **Level 1 (Content Modules):** `#FFFFFF` paired with subtle edge definition (`border: 1px solid #E5E7EB`) and diffuse ambient shadow (`0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.03)`).
- **Level 2 (Active / Raised):** `#FFFFFF` lifted by `0px 16px 32px -6px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.08)`.

### Decorative Signal Motif
Sections incorporate a low-opacity SVG background lattice based on regular hexagons, positioned as watermark-level network nodes (`rgba(10, 108, 255, 0.04)` on light fields, `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.06)` on deep navy).

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp industrial discipline with refined usability. Components utilize a calibrated `roundedness` rating of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` base radii), expanding to `0.75rem` (`12px`) for macro modules and high-priority click targets.

- **Buttons & Interactive Controls:** `8px` to `12px` border radius.
- **Cards, Panels, and Viewports:** `12px` to `16px` border radius.
- **Badges, Tags, and Pill Counters:** Fully circular pill radius (`9999px`).
- **Data Nodes & Hexagonal Icon Plates:** Regular 6-sided geometry with 2px vertex rounding to preserve technical sharpness without harsh corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Background consists of a 135-degree linear gradient from `#00E5FF` to `#0A6CFF`. High-contrast white text (`#FFFFFF`, `label-md`), `10px` border-radius, `12px 28px` padding. Transitions on hover with a concentrated shadow expansion (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.45)`) and slight color shift towards `#0047C7`.
- **Secondary Dark Surface:** Background is semi-transparent navy (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), framed by a hairline border (`rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.4)`), text color `#00E5FF`. Hover initiates a solid shift to `#0A6CFF` with white text.
- **Secondary Light Surface:** `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E5E7EB`, text `#0B0F19`. Hover state shifts border to `#0A6CFF` and text to `#0A6CFF`.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Height fixed at `48px`, border-radius `8px`, inset padding `0 16px`.
- **Light Theme Variant:** White background, `1px solid #D1D5DB`, placeholder `#9CA3AF`, active text `#0B0F19`. On focus: `1px solid #0A6CFF` with a subtle focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(10, 108, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Dark Theme Variant:** Fill `rgba(0, 24, 58, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F9FAFB`. On focus: border shifts to `#00E5FF` with a cyan ambient ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.18)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** `18px x 18px`, `4px` corner radius. Inactive: hairline border. Checked: gradient fill (`#00E5FF` to `#0A6CFF`) with a crisp white check vector mark.
- **Radio Buttons:** `18px x 18px` circle. Active: cyan outer perimeter enclosing an inner active pip of `#0A6CFF`.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- **Content Cards (Light Mode):** Rendered on clean `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px solid #E5E7EB` outline, `12px` border radius, and internal padding of `24px`.
- **Telemetry / System Modules (Dark Mode):** Rendered on `#00183A` with top edge hairline illumination (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.35)`, remaining borders `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`). Contains structural node accents or subtle hexagonal mesh watermarks in the upper right quadrant.

### Status Chips & Eyebrows
- **Structure:** `6px 12px` padding, pill-shaped (`9999px`).
- **Dark Telemetry Chip:** `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.08)` background, `1px solid rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.25)` border, text `#00E5FF`, paired with an animated pulsing LED-dot indicator (`6px x 6px`).
- **Light Category Chip:** `#F3F4F6` background, `#374151` label text, hover state brings text to `#0A6CFF`.
